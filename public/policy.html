Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This page explains what information Short Book stores and how it's used. We keep this simple: the site is built to help you create and read stories, not to collect data about you.

What's stored on your device

While you're building a story, the pictures, text and progress you enter are kept in your browser's local storage on your own device, so your work is still there if you come back later. This information stays on your device and is not automatically sent anywhere else.

What's stored on our server

Stories that are published to the shop are saved so that other readers can open and read them. We don't collect names, emails, or account details to use the site.

Third-party links

Some pages link out to WhatsApp or to third-party image generator websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which we'd encourage you to check if you use them.

Children's privacy

Short Book does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, please contact us using the details below and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will be revised.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or a request to remove a story? Message us on WhatsApp.
