Information

A quick guide to how Short Book fits together.

1. Create

Start on the home page and upload character pictures, then move to the drawing page to lay out panels and write speech bubbles for your story.

2. Read

Finished stories can be read page by page, with each speech bubble read aloud using your browser's built-in narration.

3. Shop

Browse stories other people have published in the shop. Shop stories open in a read-only view - only the story's author can edit it.

Questions?
