About Short Book

Short Book is a place to create your own illustrated short stories and comics - upload character pictures, write the dialogue, and turn it into a page-by-page story that can be read aloud right in the browser.

What you can do here

Build a story panel by panel on the create page, add speech bubbles for your characters, and preview it as a finished comic. Finished stories can be shared in the shop for others to read.

Who it's for

Short Book is built for anyone who enjoys telling stories - writers, artists, and readers who want a simple way to turn a picture and some dialogue into something worth reading.
